10 Fairytale Castles in Germany You Must Visit

ember_quest
03.07.2026

Germany is a land where history, myth, and architecture collide. With over 20,000 castles and palaces scattered across its diverse landscapes, it is no wonder that the country served as the primary inspiration for many of the Brothers Grimm’s most famous stories. Whether you are planning the ultimate 14-day Western Europe itinerary for first-timers or simply looking to step into the pages of a storybook, these fairytale fortresses are essential stops for any traveler.

1. Neuschwanstein Castle, Bavaria

Often referred to as the "Castle of the Fairy-Tale King," Neuschwanstein is the most iconic castle in the world. Built by King Ludwig II, this Romanesque Revival masterpiece served as the inspiration for Walt Disney’s Sleeping Beauty castle. Its soaring towers and dramatic position overlooking the Hohenschwangau valley make it an unmissable sight.

2. Eltz Castle, Rhineland-Palatinate

Hidden deep within a lush forest near Wierschem, Burg Eltz is a medieval marvel that has remained in the same family for over 800 years. Unlike many other castles, it was never destroyed by war, preserving its original 12th-century structure. It is particularly magical during the cooler months, making it a perfect spot if you are looking for where to go in Europe to see the best autumn foliage.

3. Hohenzollern Castle, Baden-Württemberg

Perched atop Mount Hohenzollern, this castle offers breathtaking panoramic views of the Swabian Jura. It is the ancestral seat of the Prussian Royal House and feels like a fortress suspended in the clouds. The dramatic ramparts and gothic spires create a silhouette that looks spectacular at sunrise.

4. Schwerin Castle, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern

Located on an island in Lake Schwerin, this castle is often called the "Neuschwanstein of the North." With its golden domes and sprawling orangery, it is a quintessential example of Romantic Historicism. The surrounding gardens are meticulously maintained and offer peaceful walking paths for visitors.

5. Lichtenstein Castle, Baden-Württemberg

Known as "the fairytale castle of Württemberg," Lichtenstein is a neo-Gothic dream built on a limestone cliff. Its dramatic bridge entrance is legendary, and its precarious position high above the Echaz Valley makes it a photographer’s paradise.

6. Burg Rheinstein, Rhineland-Palatinate

Overlooking the Rhine River, this castle is a quintessential romantic ruin restored to its former glory. It offers intimate tours and a terrace restaurant that provides some of the best views of the Rhine Gorge. 7. Wartburg Castle, Thuringia

A UNESCO World Heritage site, Wartburg Castle is steeped in history. It is where Martin Luther translated the New Testament into German and is famous for its role in the legend of Saint Elizabeth of Hungary. Its architecture spans nearly a thousand years of German history.

8. Drachenburg Castle, North Rhine-Westphalia

Located on the Drachenfels hill, this castle looks like it was plucked directly from a Victorian fantasy. While it may look medieval, it was actually built in the late 19th century as a private villa. The interiors are lavishly decorated, showcasing the wealth of the industrial era.

9. Mespelbrunn Castle, Bavaria

Tucked away in a secluded valley in the Spessart forest, Mespelbrunn is a water castle that feels frozen in time. Because of its remote location, it was spared during the various conflicts that ravaged Germany, allowing it to maintain its Renaissance charm.

10. Cochem Castle (Reichsburg), Rhineland-Palatinate

Dominating the skyline of the town of Cochem, this hilltop castle was restored in the 19th century in the neo-Gothic style. It features high walls and turrets that look stunning against the backdrop of the Moselle River vineyards.

Tips for Visiting

  • Book tickets for popular castles like Neuschwanstein months in advance.
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes, as most castles involve steep climbs or cobblestone paths.
  • Check opening hours, as many smaller castles have limited schedules during the off-season.
  • Consider a regional rail pass to save money if you plan to visit multiple castles in one state.
