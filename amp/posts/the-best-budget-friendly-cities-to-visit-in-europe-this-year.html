The Best Budget-Friendly Cities to Visit in Europe This Year

cinder_shade
07.06.2026

Europe often carries a reputation for being an expensive destination, but savvy travelers know that the continent is filled with hidden gems where your money stretches significantly further. From the sun-drenched coastlines of the Balkans to the historic charm of Central Europe, finding affordable destinations is all about looking beyond the typical tourist traps of Paris or London. If you are wondering how to plan a 2-week Europe trip on a budget, choosing the right base is the most critical first step.

1. Sofia, Bulgaria

Sofia is consistently ranked as one of the most affordable capital cities in Europe. Nestled at the foot of Vitosha Mountain, this city offers a unique blend of Roman ruins, Ottoman-era mosques, and Soviet-style architecture. The cost of dining out in Sofia is remarkably low compared to Western European standards, allowing travelers to enjoy hearty Bulgarian cuisine without breaking the bank.

  • Must-see: Alexander Nevsky Cathedral.
  • Budget Tip: Take advantage of the free Sofia walking tours to learn about the city’s complex history.

2. Krakow, Poland

Krakow is a fairytale destination that feels far more expensive than it actually is. With its well-preserved medieval core and the vibrant Jewish Quarter of Kazimierz, the city offers a rich cultural experience. Poland remains one of the best-value destinations in the EU, offering high-quality beer, affordable pierogi, and a wealth of history for a fraction of the cost of cities like Prague or Vienna.

Why Krakow Wins on Price

Accommodation in Krakow is plentiful, ranging from boutique hostels to luxury apartments that remain accessible for mid-range budgets. Public transportation is efficient and inexpensive, meaning you can navigate the city’s main attractions without needing to rely on costly taxis.

3. Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina

For those looking for a destination that offers a profound cultural experience and incredible value, Sarajevo is the place to be. Known as the "Jerusalem of Europe" due to its religious diversity, the city is surrounded by mountains and steeped in history. The local coffee culture is not only a way of life but also incredibly affordable.

4. Budapest, Hungary

While Budapest has gained popularity in recent years, it remains a fantastic budget option. Famous for its thermal baths and "ruin bars," the city provides an unmatched nightlife scene. By staying on the Buda side of the river, you can often find more affordable accommodation while still being within walking distance of the major sights.

  • Budget Activity: Spend an afternoon soaking in the Széchenyi Thermal Baths.
  • Dining: Look for "étkezde" (canteen-style restaurants) for authentic, cheap lunches.

Final Tips for Your Budget European Adventure

Traveling on a budget doesn't mean you have to sacrifice quality. By focusing on these Eastern and Central European destinations, you can extend your stay and see more of the world. Remember to book your regional trains in advance, consider traveling during the shoulder season (May/June or September/October), and always opt for local street food to get the best taste of the culture while saving your budget for experiences that truly matter.
