Where to Go in Europe to See the Best Autumn Foliage

midnight_pulse
23.06.2026 • Read time: 8 min

As the sweltering heat of summer fades and the crisp, cool air settles over the continent, Europe transforms into a breathtaking canvas of crimson, gold, and burnt orange. Autumn is arguably the most magical time to explore the Old World; the tourist crowds have thinned, the light is golden and soft, and the landscapes are draped in vibrant seasonal hues. Whether you are a photography enthusiast or simply seeking a tranquil escape, knowing exactly where to find the best foliage is key to an unforgettable journey.

The Best Regions for Autumn Colors in Europe

Europe offers a diverse array of climates and terrains, meaning you can chase the foliage from late September through November. From the rugged peaks of the Alps to the rolling vineyards of Tuscany, here are the top destinations to witness the changing of the seasons.

1. The Bavarian Alps, Germany

Bavaria is the quintessential autumn destination. As the deciduous forests surrounding the fairytale Neuschwanstein Castle turn vibrant shades of amber, the contrast against the snow-dusted limestone peaks is unparalleled. The region is perfect for those who want to experience Europe by rail, as the train journeys through the alpine valleys offer panoramic views of the changing canopy that you simply cannot get from a car.

2. The Loire Valley, France

Known as the "Garden of France," the Loire Valley is home to hundreds of châteaux surrounded by vast forests and manicured gardens. In October, the vineyards turn a deep, rusted gold, providing a stunning backdrop to the limestone architecture. It is an ideal spot for a leisurely road trip or a cycling tour through the countryside.

3. The Dolomites, Italy

The Italian Dolomites offer a unique autumn experience. The larch forests here turn a brilliant, glowing yellow, which looks spectacular against the jagged, pale rock formations. Hiking in the Dolomites during October is a sensory delight, with cool temperatures and clear skies providing the perfect conditions for outdoor adventure.

Planning Your Autumn Escape

Traveling during the shoulder season is a smart move for your wallet, but it still requires careful preparation. Essential Tips for Autumn Travel

  • Dress in Layers: The weather in Europe during autumn can be unpredictable. A light sweater paired with a waterproof jacket is essential.
  • Book in Advance: While summer crowds are gone, popular leaf-peeping spots can still get busy on weekends.
  • Photography Gear: Bring a polarizer filter for your camera to make those autumn colors pop against the blue sky.
  • Payment Methods: To avoid unnecessary costs while hopping between countries, ensure you are using top travel credit cards with no foreign transaction fees for Europe to maximize your spending power.

Autumn in Europe is not just about the scenery; it is about the pace of life. It is a time for warm beverages in cozy cafes, long walks through rustling forests, and appreciating the quiet beauty of the continent before the winter snows arrive. By choosing the right destination at the right time, you are guaranteed a front-row seat to one of nature’s most spectacular performances.
