The Ultimate 14-Day Western Europe Itinerary for First-Timers

midnight_pulse
13.06.2026 • Read time: 5 min

Planning your first trip to Western Europe can feel like a daunting task. With a rich tapestry of history, world-class gastronomy, and iconic landmarks, the continent offers an overwhelming number of choices. To make the most of your two weeks, a well-structured itinerary is essential to ensure you balance sightseeing with genuine immersion. Before you depart, consider checking out these top travel credit cards with no foreign transaction fees for Europe to save money on currency conversion charges while you roam.

The Golden Route: London, Paris, and Amsterdam

For first-timers, the classic London-Paris-Amsterdam circuit provides the perfect introduction to European culture, efficient rail travel, and historic charm. This route is beginner-friendly and requires minimal flying, allowing you to maximize your time on the ground.

Days 1-4: London, United Kingdom

Start your adventure in the vibrant capital of England. Spend your first few days exploring the Tower of London, the British Museum, and the South Bank. If you are looking for ways to keep costs low, read up on the best budget-friendly cities to visit in Europe this year to see how your budget might stretch further in different regions.

  • Day 1: Arrival and West End exploration.
  • Day 2: Westminster Abbey, Big Ben, and the London Eye.
  • Day 3: The British Museum and Covent Garden.
  • Day 4: Morning market visit at Borough Market before catching the Eurostar train to Paris.

Days 5-9: Paris, France

Paris is the heart of Western Europe. Beyond the Eiffel Tower, ensure you lose yourself in the streets of Le Marais and the artistic history of Montmartre. Paris is a city that rewards walking, so pack comfortable shoes.

When planning your trip, it is helpful to understand the real-world costs of travel. For a breakdown of expenses, you can consult this guide on what a 10-day trip to Europe actually costs to ensure your financial planning is realistic for a 14-day duration.

Days 10-14: Amsterdam, Netherlands

Conclude your trip in the "Venice of the North." Amsterdam’s canal rings are a UNESCO World Heritage site, offering a serene contrast to the bustle of London and Paris. If you plan to extend your travels to include other regions, you might be interested in researching the top UNESCO World Heritage sites in Eastern Europe for future inspiration.

Traveling across borders requires preparation. Whether you are using a Eurail pass or booking individual tickets, always book your high-speed trains in advance to secure the best rates.

Sustainable and Smart Travel Tips

As a first-timer, it is important to remember to be a conscious traveler. Popular destinations like Venice are currently dealing with overtourism; if you decide to add Italy to your itinerary, please learn how to visit Venice responsibly without contributing to overtourism.

Finally, preparation is the key to a stress-free vacation. Before you head to the airport, review these 10 essential tips for backpacking across Europe, which contain universal advice on packing light, staying safe, and navigating public transit like a local.

Packing Checklist for Your 14-Day Trip

  • Universal power adapter (Type C/F).
  • Comfortable walking shoes (non-negotiable!).
  • A portable power bank for long days of sightseeing.
  • A physical copy of your passport and travel insurance.
  • A lightweight rain jacket for unpredictable European weather.
